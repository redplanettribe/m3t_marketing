# Positioning & Messaging

## High-Level Positioning

| Angle | Frame | Statement |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| **Who is this for** — Workflow | people who are… | running events on a regular basis |
| **Who is this for** — Competitive Alternative | using… | a frankenstein of traditional tools |
| **Who is this for** — Problem | and that sucks because… | massive admin burden |
| **What it is** — Category | we are a… | conference operations platform |
| **Why is it better** — Differentiation | here's what we do differently | low stress, high efficiency event day, easy analytics |

---

## Supporting Arguments

Each argument follows the same structure:
**Step** (what you do) → **Competitive Alternative** (how it's done today) → **Sub Problem** (why that sucks) → **Feature** → **Capability** → **Benefit**.

### 1. Deliverables

- **Step:** give stuff
- **Competitive Alternative:** at the entrance or, later, with a printed list
- **Sub Problem:** massive bottlenecks, obstruction of flow
- **Feature:** deliverable system
- **Capability:** deliver asynchronously and keep track of it — scan QR code and deliver stuff
- **Benefit:** no registration bottlenecks; no risk of delivering things twice; no more long queues; keep track of what and how much you deliver

### 2. Session Booking

- **Step:** tickets booking system
- **Competitive Alternative:** give tickets to individual sessions
- **Sub Problem:** bottlenecks to pick tickets; empty seats; admin headache; attendee frustration
- **Feature:** booking system
- **Capability:** allow attendees to book and cancel booking of individual sessions according to capacity
- **Benefit:** attendees know what sessions have available spots; attendees make their own agenda and know where to go next

### 3. Communications

- **Step:** communicate with the attendees
- **Competitive Alternative:** have staff running around herding people
- **Sub Problem:** it's impossible to reach all people having staff running around — you are going to reach some but not most
- **Feature:** chat and announcements
- **Capability:** general chat where people can interact with organizers and other attendees; announcements broadcasted to all attendees' notifications
- **Benefit:** attendees are very updated, either by organizers or other attendees

### 4. Sponsor Reports

- **Step:** compile data to prove value to sponsors
- **Competitive Alternative:** compile event info
- **Sub Problem:** a very hard and manual labor — the more sponsors the more work; very hard to prove value without data
- **Feature:** sponsor interactions and reports
- **Capability:** virtual booths where attendees interact with sponsors and their offerings; engagement tracking and automatic reports per sponsor
- **Benefit:** attendees have an easy way to find sponsors and interact; automatic reports to prove value to each individual sponsor

### 5. Networking

- **Step:** actividades de networking (networking activities)
- **Competitive Alternative:** organize, communicate, explain, moderate
- **Sub Problem:** extremely hard to organize; small proportion of attendees take part in the activities; almost impossible to quantify
- **Feature:** networking & discoverability
- **Capability:** networking section to discover and interact
- **Benefit:** attendees have an easy way to discover people and interact; we keep track of all interactions

### 6. Impact Tracking

- **Step:** impact report
- **Competitive Alternative:** compile previous individual events reports into one big report
- **Sub Problem:** very labor intensive; takes time finding and compiling info
- **Feature:** automatic org impact reports
- **Capability:** allow org organizers to generate an automatic impact report
- **Benefit:** automatic, instant report of all the impact the org has had
