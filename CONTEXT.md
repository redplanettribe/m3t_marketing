# Multiticketing Marketing Site

The marketing site for Multiticketing, a conference operations platform. Its job is to explain the product and drive visitors into the Dashboard app.

## Language

**Dashboard**:
The Multiticketing app itself, hosted at manager.multiticketing.com, where organizers manage events. Referred to on the marketing site only through the "Start" CTA.
_Avoid_: Manager app, admin panel

**Start**:
The primary CTA. Links directly to the Dashboard signup, open to anyone — no approval step. See [[0001-open-self-serve-signup]].
_Avoid_: Request access, Get access, Sign up (reserve "Sign up" for in-app copy, not this marketing CTA)
